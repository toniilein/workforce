## Details
Key decision: Years 1-3 are project-based economics. The standardized platform economics come out of those projects; do not model the early company as though the finished factory already exists.

## Actions
- Split Lionscraft costs into:
  - fixed platform/company costs;
  - variable project costs.
- Every early facility/project must cover:
  - its external structuring/modelling/legal/SPV/etc. costs;
  - its share of Lionscraft delivery effort;
  - a proper margin.
- Build the first 2-3 years around project-based pricing.
- Then model standardisation/licensing/recurring platform economics increasingly taking over in Y4-Y5.
- Calculate:
  - fixed annual cost base;
  - contribution per project;
  - required project volume to cover the fixed base;
  - gross/project margin;
  - break-even volume.
