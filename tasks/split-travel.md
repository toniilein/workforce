## Notes

### Split, Croatia — Last Week of August 2026

**Weather:** Peak summer season. Expect 28-35°C, crowded but all restaurants/bars open. Book ahead.

---

### Top Restaurants

**Local & Authentic Croatian**
- **Villa Spiza** — Family-run, cash only, handwritten menu, seasonal dishes. Best shrimp, spaghetti, beef steak, fried smelts. No reservations — go early or weekday.
- **Konoba Kod Joze** — "The place tour bus drivers go." Authentic Dalmatian pasticada (slow-cooked beef in wine/prunes), local beers. Quiet at 4pm.
- **Buffet Fife** — Hearty, no-frills Dalmatian. Famous black risotto, grilled fish. Local favorite near Riva.
- **Uje Oil Bar** — Olive oil tastings + seasonal Dalmatian dishes. Cuttlefish, prawn & sea fennel, charcuterie boards.

**Fine Dining**
- **Storija Fine Dining** — Only restaurant inside Diocletian's Palace walls. Vaulted stone ceilings, locally sourced, fantastic service. On the Riva.
- **Zrno Soli** — "Grain of Salt." Imaginative dishes, marina views. Michelin-listed.
- **Leonis** — Spectacular seafood, don't miss the squid ink risotto.
- **Chops Grill** — Luxury steakhouse, quiet escape on edge of Old Town.

**International**
- **To Je Tako** — Tiny Mexican (20 seats, alley off Diocletian's Palace). Best nachos, tacos, enchiladas. Chipotle/Jalapeno Margaritas. Book via Facebook.
- **Silk** — Pan-Asian. Ramen, sushi, katsu curry. Pricey but consistent.
- **Pizzeria Bokamorra** — Neapolitan-style pizza, dessert pizzas (white chocolate + raspberry + pistachio). Instagram-worthy.
- **Pizzeria Skipper** — Cheap pizza with marina sunset views.

**Breakfast / Coffee / Gelato**
- **Brasserie 7** — Best brunch in town, right on the Riva.
- **kava2** — Best specialty coffee + almond croissants. Minimalist vibe.
- **Sladoledarnica Emiliana** — Best gelato in Split. Queue speaks for itself.
- **Luka Ice Cream & Cakes** — Local favorite, massive portions. Try lavender flavor.
- **ST Burek** — Authentic burek (filo pastry with cheese/meat/spinach). Great street snack.

**Drinks**
- **Zinfandel Wine Bar** — Local wine tasting (3 wines + meat/cheese pairings). Dalmatia is the origin of Zinfandel.
- **Bokeria Kitchen & Wine Bar** — Sophisticated wine bar, vaulted ceilings.
- **Paradox Wine & Cheese Bar** — Most interesting wine list in Split. Indigenous grapes: Plavac Mali, Posip + Pag cheese & prsut.
- **The Daltonist** — Craft cocktails, craft beers. Stone vaulted ceilings, cozy. Popular with expats.
- **Marvlvs Library Jazz Bar** — Live music, books, atmospheric. Birthplace of poet Marko Marulic.
- **Kavana Central** — Hidden rooftop terrace in Narodni Trg Square. Terracotta rooftop views.

### Suggested 4-Day Itinerary (Aug 24-27)

**Day 1 — Arrive + Old Town**
- Morning: Arrive, check in
- Afternoon: Walk Diocletian's Palace (free). See the four Roman gates (Golden, Silver, Iron, Brass). Rub Bishop Gregory of Nin's golden toe at Golden Gate.
- Late afternoon: Climb Cathedral of Saint Domnius bell tower (EUR7, 183 steps). Best views in Split.
- Evening: Dinner at Villa Spiza -> drinks at Zinfandel Wine Bar
- Night: Walk the Riva promenade

**Day 2 — Palace Deep Dive + Beach**
- Morning: Cathedral of Saint Domnius (EUR7, combined with bell tower if not done). See 1214 carved wooden doors, Roman frieze with Diocletian portraits.
- Midday: Vestibule — listen to klapa singers (a cappella Dalmatian choral, UNESCO heritage). Free (tip EUR1-2). Best 11am-1pm.
- Afternoon: Bacvice Beach (sand beach, lively) or Kasjuni Beach (clear water, relaxed vibe)
- Evening: Dinner at Konoba Kod Joze -> gelato at Sladoledarnica Emiliana
- Night: Marvlvs Library Jazz Bar for live music

**Day 3 — Island Day Trip: Hvar**
- Morning: Ferry to Hvar Island (~1.5 hours). Book ahead in peak season.
- Day: Explore Hvar town, Spanish Fortress (EUR7, sunset views), Stari Grad (lavender fields if any left in late Aug)
- Lunch: Fig Hvar (best Croatian on island) or Alviz (best pizza)
- Beach: Falko Beach Bar for sunset views
- Evening: Return ferry -> dinner at Uje Oil Bar or To Je Tako

**Day 4 — Marjan Hill + Trogir**
- Morning: Climb Marjan Hill (best sunrise/sunset views over Split and islands)
- Midday: Coffee at kava2, burek at ST Burek
- Afternoon: Day trip to Trogir (30 min bus) — UNESCO medieval walled town
- Evening: Farewell dinner at Storija Fine Dining (inside palace walls) or Zrno Soli (marina views)
- Night: The Daltonist for craft cocktails

**Bonus Day 5 — Krka National Park**
- Full day trip to Krka waterfalls (~1.5h from Split). Swim at Skradinski Buk waterfall. Less crowded than Plitvice.

---

### Practical Tips

- **Cash:** Many places (Villa Spiza, To Je Tako) are cash only. Carry euros.
- **Reservations:** Essential in peak season (August), especially for fine dining and small spots.
- **Ferries:** Book Hvar/Brac ferries in advance. Krilo speedboat (~EUR25 one-way).
- **Walking shoes:** Palace streets are ancient stone, uneven. Bell tower stairs are steep and open-air.
- **Timing:** Late August is still peak — arrive early at popular restaurants, expect queues at gelato spots.
