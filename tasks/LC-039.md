## Details
Evaluate whether the Lionscraft facility architecture can function without a Special Purpose Vehicle (SPV).

## Questions to answer
- What does the SPV currently do in our proposed structure? (bankruptcy remoteness, ring-fencing, legal isolation, capital holding)
- What regulatory or legal requirements actually mandate an SPV for parametric risk transfer?
- Can smart contracts / on-chain structures replace the legal isolation function of an SPV?
- What are the trade-offs: cost, complexity, speed to market, investor comfort, regulatory acceptance?
- Are there jurisdictions or structures (e.g. cell companies, protected cell structures, direct balance sheet) that remove the need for a full SPV?
- What do other players in the parametric / ILS space do? Do any operate without SPVs?

## Output
A short assessment: SPV required, SPV optional with modifications, or SPV can be eliminated. Include implications for cost, speed, and investor/recipient acceptance.
